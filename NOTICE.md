# Attribution & honesty note

This repository documents my actual working set of AI-engineering skills and review
agents. In the spirit of not overclaiming:

- **Most of the skills and every review agent here were built from our own
  production incidents** — each one traces to a specific class of bug that cost real
  time, and is written up as such.
- **A subset are community skills I adopted and adapted** into my workflow, rather
  than authored from scratch. Where a pattern originates with a third-party author
  or an open-source project, that origin is noted in the skill itself, and credit
  belongs to the original author.

The point of the collection isn't "I invented all of these." It's "this is the real
system I run every day, and here's how it's put together" — some of it original,
some of it adapted, all of it in genuine use.

Everything is sanitized: client names, hosts, secrets, and product specifics are
removed. The patterns are general and reusable.
